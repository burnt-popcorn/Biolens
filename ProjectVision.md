# BioLens

## Problem
Upload FASTA files and analyze the GC content, codon content, protein content, ORF predictions etc etc.

## Users
By Anyone who is curious but mainly bio research students/individuals for visualizing fasta files clearly with graphs/charts.

## Features
A detailed log of the break down of said filed, analytics, clear visual aids
1. species identification.
2. disease identifcation/ risks of diseases
3. perhaps ancestry
ORF -> open reading frame- single stretch of DNA that contains 3 letters representing an Amino acid. It can read the cell and essentially translate it.
